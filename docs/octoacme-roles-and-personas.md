# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Leads / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads coordinate engineering execution across the team, translate priorities into actionable work, and help unblock delivery while maintaining quality and predictability.

### Responsibilities
- Sequence work across squads or functions to support milestone delivery
- Help define sprint or iteration priorities with the PM and Product Manager
- Identify technical dependencies, delivery risks, and cross-team blockers
- Support engineering planning, capacity discussions, and execution trade-offs
- Partner with developers and QA to ensure work is ready for review and release

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery flow and team focus
- Reduce execution bottlenecks and coordination overhead
- Improve delivery quality without sacrificing velocity

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning, daily standups, and milestone reviews
- Dependency tracking and escalation updates
- Technical planning discussions with product and PM stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to monitor schedule and dependencies
- Aligns with Product Managers on sequencing and trade-offs for priority changes
- Supports Developers by clarifying implementation dependencies and delivery constraints
- Coordinates with QA/Release partners to ensure work is ready for validation and deployment

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and compliance partners help ensure projects meet required controls, standards, and risk expectations while minimizing avoidable operational and regulatory exposure.

### Responsibilities
- Review features, integrations, and deployments for security and compliance risk
- Define approval gates, review checkpoints, and control requirements
- Partner with engineering and PMs to mitigate risks early in the lifecycle
- Support incident review, risk escalation, and remediation planning
- Ensure project work aligns with applicable organizational policies and standards

### Goals
- Reduce preventable security and compliance issues
- Improve trust and readiness for release and production use
- Embed risk awareness into project planning and delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints, design walkthroughs, and risk assessments
- Escalation updates during high-risk or incident-driven work
- Sign-off checkpoints for release or deployment readiness

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers to identify risk and regulatory impacts early
- Advises Developers on secure design, testing, and remediation practices
- Coordinates with Release Managers and QA to confirm controls are in place before production
- Escalates critical findings to sponsors and stakeholders when business impact is significant

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Enablement Leads help ensure delivery is aligned with adoption, stakeholder readiness, and real-world user outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Translate customer or stakeholder needs into delivery feedback and readiness requirements
- Coordinate adoption, training, and communication planning for new or changed capabilities
- Monitor user feedback, support trends, and service readiness signals
- Partner with Product Managers to ensure customer impact is considered in prioritization
- Assist with release communication, rollout readiness, and feedback loops

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce friction during rollout and change management
- Ensure teams understand how new capabilities affect users and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder check-ins, customer feedback reviews, and readiness sessions
- Release communications and adoption updates
- Product and roadmap alignment discussions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on customer value and experience priorities
- Works with Project Managers to align rollout schedules and stakeholder communications
- Supports Developers and QA with edge-case feedback and validation scenarios
- Helps PMs and sponsors communicate impact and readiness at milestone reviews

---

## Release Managers / QA Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers and QA Leads ensure that software is validated, deployment-ready, and supported through a reliable release process.

### Responsibilities
- Define and coordinate release readiness checks and deployment sequencing
- Manage smoke tests, validation plans, and acceptance verification
- Coordinate with engineering, PMs, and stakeholders on release windows and follow-up
- Track defects, release blockers, and post-deployment issues
- Support rollback, incident triage, and communication when needed

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and customer disruption
- Maintain a reliable quality gate before deployment
- Improve transparency into release status and issues

### Typical Communication
- Release planning, validation checkpoints, and launch reviews
- Defect triage and issue escalation communication
- Post-release status updates and retrospective notes

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers and Delivery Leads on readiness and defect closure
- Works with Project Managers to confirm release plans and stakeholder communications
- Coordinates with Security/Compliance partners for risk review before deployment
- Supports Product Managers in validating that outcomes and acceptance criteria are met

---

## Data / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners help define and track the metrics that show whether a project is delivering the intended value and whether operational risks are being managed effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Identify the key success metrics, dashboards, and data sources for the project
- Define baseline measurements and track progress toward milestone or release outcomes
- Partner with Product Managers to evaluate whether business goals are being achieved
- Highlight risks, anomalies, or gaps in reporting that require action
- Support post-release analysis and continuous improvement efforts

### Goals
- Make project value measurable and visible
- Improve evidence-based decision making across the lifecycle
- Support learning and iteration based on trends and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Metric reviews, dashboard updates, and stakeholder reporting
- Analysis summaries for milestone and release reviews
- Follow-up on the impacts of delivery and adoption trends

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides Product Managers with outcome data to validate priorities and goals
- Helps Project Managers report progress, risks, and dependencies with evidence
- Works with Developers and QA to track quality-related metrics and adoption signals
- Supplies Customer Success and stakeholder teams with impact data for communications

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help teams model cross-functional ownership, improve accountability, and clarify how work flows across product, engineering, QA, release, security, and stakeholder-facing functions.
